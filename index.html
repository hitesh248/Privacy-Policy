1. Who we are

This policy applies to all Android applications published on Google Play by NextZen Solution ("we", "us", "the Apps"). It explains what information the Apps handle, why, and the choices you have.

2. Data stored only on your device

The things you create or keep in our Apps — for example habits and history, expenses and budgets, hidden photos, videos, files and notes, recovered messages, settings and backups — are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server operated by us, and we cannot access them.

  • Backups are created only when you choose, as a file in a location you select. Some Apps let you protect backups with a password.
  • Uninstalling an App deletes its on-device data (except backup files you saved elsewhere).
  • Because we don't hold this data, we cannot recover it if it is lost.

3. Data collected by third-party services

To show ads, understand app performance and fix crashes, the Apps use the services below. They may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app instance ID
  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, language, app version
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location)
  • App interactions: screens viewed, features used, ad interactions
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

These never include the content you create in the Apps (such as habit names, expenses, hidden files or messages).

4. Ads and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are personalized, the App asks for your consent. You can change your choice anytime from Privacy options in the App's settings.
  • Everywhere: you can limit ad personalization in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Free versions of the Apps will still show ads, but they may be less relevant to you.
  • Premium versions of some Apps remove ads.

5. Purchases

Paid features and subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card or bank details. We receive purchase information (such as which product was bought and whether a subscription is active) so the App can unlock what you paid for. See also our Terms of Service.

6. App-specific details

Some Apps need special permissions to work. Permissions are requested only for the features that use them, and you can revoke them anytime in your phone's settings.

Habiro – Habit Tracker

  • Notifications & alarms: to deliver habit reminders on time.
  • Calendar (optional): creates a local "Habiro" calendar on your device for your habits. It is not synced to any online account by the App.
  • Photos (optional): you pick photos with the Android photo picker for "reasons to quit"; a copy is kept only in the App, with location data removed.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock (optional): for app lock and backup password recovery. Verification is done by Android; the App never sees your biometric data or PIN.

Expense Tracker: Money Manager

  • Your expenses, budgets and reports are stored only on your device. The App does not connect to your bank.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set.

Mobile Data Tracker & Monitor

  • Usage access: to read your device's data usage statistics per app. This information is shown on your device only.
  • Phone state & network state: to show network type, carrier and SIM-specific usage.
  • Notifications: for the live data usage notification and data limit alerts.
  • Speed test: runs a download/upload test against Cloudflare's speed test servers, which receive your IP address as part of any internet connection (Cloudflare policy).

Calculator Vault Pro

  • Photos, videos and files access: to move the items you choose into the vault and remove the originals from their public location.
  • Vault content is encrypted and stored only on your device.
  • Camera (optional, intruder alert): takes a photo when someone enters a wrong PIN or opens the decoy vault. Photos stay on your device.
  • Microphone (optional): only for voice-to-text in notes, when you use it.
  • Private browser: does not save history or cookies after you close it. Websites you visit have their own privacy policies.

AppLock Pro

  • Usage access: to detect when a locked app is opened, so the lock screen can appear. This happens on your device only.
  • Display over other apps: to show the lock screen on top of locked apps.
  • Photos, videos and files access (optional): to hide the items you choose and remove the originals from their public location.
  • Camera (optional, intruder alert) and fingerprint: handled on your device; biometric data is never accessed by the App.

WhatsDelete: Recover Messages

  • Notification access: with your permission, the App reads notifications from the messaging apps you select, so it can show messages that are later deleted. Message text and sender names are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Media and status saving: statuses and media you choose to save are stored on your device.
  • You can turn off notification access anytime in your phone's settings, and delete saved data from within the App.
  • WhatsDelete is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

Battery Charging Animation Art

  • Display over other apps: to show the charging animation when your phone is plugged in.
  • Battery level and charging state are read on your device only.
  • Wallpapers: when you choose a wallpaper, the App sets it on your device using Android's wallpaper feature. Wallpapers you apply or save stay on your device.

Calendar – Tasks & Reminder

  • Your tasks, events and reminders are stored on your device.
  • Notifications & alarms: to deliver reminders on time.

MP3 Music Player & Equalizer

  • Audio files access: to find and play music stored on your device. Your music files are not uploaded.
  • Notifications: for playback controls.

Pipe Connect – Logic Puzzle

  • Game progress is stored on your device. Optional rewarded ads give in-game rewards (such as hints) after an ad is watched.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above for the purposes described, or when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to investigate fraud.

8. Retention and security

  • On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their retention settings (for analytics and crash data, typically up to 14 months; advertising data according to the ad providers' policies).
  • We use reasonable measures to protect information, such as encryption for vault content and password-protected backups, but no method is 100% secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • Most data stays on your device, so you can delete it yourself in the App or by uninstalling it.
  • For data held by third-party services, use the controls in section 4, or email us with your request and we will help.
  • Where we rely on consent (personalized ads in the EEA/UK), you can withdraw it anytime from Privacy options in the App.

10. Children

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our Apps change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be reflected before the App version they relate to is released.
